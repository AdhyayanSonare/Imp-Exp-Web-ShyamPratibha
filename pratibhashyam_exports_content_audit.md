# Pratibhashyam Exports — Website Content Scrape & Audit Handoff

**Audit date:** 18 November 2026  
**Primary website:** https://pratibhashyamexports.com/  
**Purpose:** Content inventory and source-of-truth handoff for rebuilding the website. This document records existing website content and flags content quality issues. It is not a visual redesign specification and does not prescribe button behaviour.

## 1. Scope and limitations

The audit covers the public pages linked from the main navigation, the homepage sections, and the product subpages linked from the Products section. The public sitemap endpoints could not be retrieved during this audit. The Pinterest short link supplied as a design reference also did not resolve through public access, so no assumptions about its layout are included here. Pinterest is treated only as a future visual reference, not as a source of company content.

The homepage hero is recorded below for completeness, but its redesign is outside this content handoff because the hero is being handled separately.

**Important:** The website contains claims that need confirmation by the company before they are reused as factual proof points. They are recorded as claims currently published on the site, not independently verified facts.

## 9. Content audit — what is usable, cluttered, missing or contradictory

### 9.1 Strong content worth retaining (after validation and editing)

- Clear company identity as an India-based export company.
- The export workflow from sourcing and inspection through documentation, logistics and delivery.
- Direct-sourcing relationship with farmers, FPOs and manufacturers.
- The six-step Farm-to-Container process.
- Broad product coverage and the extensive product-name inventory.
- The four director names, once each profile’s photo and responsibilities are confirmed.
- The vision and mission statements.
- Export documentation, freight/logistics and Incoterms capabilities.
- EXIM education and consultancy as a distinct business/service offering.
- Existing operational photography in the gallery, once captions and image permissions are confirmed.

### 9.2 Clutter, duplication and template residue

- The homepage opening headline crams many product categories into one long, grammatically awkward sentence.
- The homepage and About page repeat similar company positioning, sourcing, quality and global-network claims. Use one concise company introduction and reserve the deeper company story for About Us.
- The Agriculture category page contains unrelated travel/financial-services template copy. Remove it entirely.
- The Certifications section has a cut-off sentence and photographs that are not certificate evidence. Replace with genuine certificate scans and exact certificate details, or rename the section to match the real content.
- The Agriculture category index and Frozen Vegetables page have inconsistent labels/breadcrumb text.
- The About page displays two 0% metrics. These are unusable and damage credibility.
- The homepage global-presence text and the hero’s UAE/Thailand/Canada presence claim are not aligned. Clarify the difference between owned offices, representatives, partners and export destinations.
- The “Meet Our Directors” area has four images but no clear name-to-image/role mapping in the captured text.
- Product pages are long lists with little buyer-facing information beyond product names; this can become overwhelming without a better category hierarchy and meaningful product details.
- Product image labels/alt text are inconsistent with some listed products: for example, the Fruits & Vegetables page exposes “Green Cardamom” image labels alongside Fresh Onions and Green Chillies, while the Grains page exposes multiple “Cluster Beans” image labels alongside rice and grain entries. The actual photos should be checked against each product before reuse.
- Gallery photographs have no captions, context, dates or location labels; two event photographs appear near-duplicates.
- “Vission”, “Yout”, “Musterd” and several grammatical inconsistencies need correction.
- The training page mixes a dense 20-topic syllabus, service descriptions, programme claims and FAQs in one long page. Information is useful but not prioritised.
- “AD Overseas” and the email-as-website string appear to be leftover or incorrect content on the training page.

### 9.3 Contradictions and verification items

1. **Global presence:** homepage says own presence in UAE, Thailand and Canada; the Global Presence block only names Dubai/UAE office, India office and export destinations. Confirm offices versus partners/destinations and publish an accurate list.
2. **Product category count:** About Us says six export categories; the visible Product landing page has four parent categories. Agriculture is split into seven child categories. Explain the official category structure and use one consistent count.
3. **Commodity count:** homepage says 20+ commodities, while the nine listing pages show 150 named product entries. This may be because product variants are counted differently; define the metric or omit it.
4. **Farmers connected / countries served / years of experience / Google rating:** confirm current figures, source and date before publication.
